The Halloween theme has returned to the game. The menu is orange, and the background has been replaced with two versions for night and day. Halloween decorations, however, have not yet been added.
Next, MoNTE48, the game's main developer, announced the arrival of a new mod for signs, allowing for formatting options such as bold and italics, as well as color. No release date has been announced.
