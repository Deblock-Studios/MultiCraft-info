The Halloween theme is back in the game. The menu is orange, and the background has been replaced with two versions for night and day. The Halloween decoration items, however, have not arrived yet.

Next, MoNTE48, the game's lead developer, announced the arrival of a new mod for signs, allowing formatting such as bold or italic as well as color. No date announced.
