The mannequins will have a "voice"! And yes, MoNTE showed us that mannequins will be able to speak with several parameters, such as: sending messages one by one, if a player is nearby, or if you click on them, and whether you can make it repeat

And let's talk about the new chat, it's available for beta users on Android (I don't know if it's available on TestFlight on iOS) so yes, the chat is in beta, but it is available and usable
It therefore takes up the whole screen when you open it, with a "/" button that shows the last commands used (when you type "/" it puts the same thing), you can click on other people's nicknames to "mention" them, this will put their nickname in front of our message (e.g.: [croissant] aurelmattnath: command_block: how's it going?) and our messages are highlighted
