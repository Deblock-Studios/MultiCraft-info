Two recent additions:
- You can now choose between OpenGL and Vulkan (on Android) or Metal (on Apple) in the settings. As a reminder, these are low-level graphics APIs that theoretically offer a boost in FPS. Having this choice is a welcome feature, especially for devices that do not support Vulkan or Metal.
- Armor textures have been reworked. A server restart is required to see the changes.
