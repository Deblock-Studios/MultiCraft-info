Two freshly made additions:
- It is now possible to choose, in the settings, between OpenGL and Vulkan (on Android)/Metal (on Apple). As a reminder, these are low-level graphics APIs, theoretically allowing a gain in FPS. Having the option to choose is a good thing, especially for devices that are incompatible with Vulkan/Metal.
- The armor textures have been redone. Restarting the server will allow you to enjoy them.
