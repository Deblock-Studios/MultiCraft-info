Today brings several updates, including the much-awaited skin editor! We can finally create our own custom skins by drawing them directly in the interface. There are also different brush sizes for the `/ie` tool (and the skin editor), allowing for 3x3 or cross-shaped patterns, and we can even paint helicopters—something that wasn't possible before.
