MoNTE48 gave us a sneak peek at the `/ie` feature, which will allow us to mine a 5x5 area (in a cross shape) or a 3x3 area (presumably a square). It includes several options, such as breaking only the same type of block, breaking only blocks mineable with that specific tool, or breaking *all* blocks.

We’ll keep you posted as soon as this feature arrives.
