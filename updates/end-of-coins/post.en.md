The impossible is happening.
MoNTE48 publicly announced the end of coins a few moments ago.
This includes the return of the ability to create servers for free.

MoNTE48 justifies this with the developers' wish to "make the game free for players", which contrasts sharply with the many microtransactions added over the past months. We will keep you informed!
