title: "The end of multiplayer?"
---

We just learned that the Telegram channel "Actualités MultiCraft" had a brief discussion with MoNTE48 about the future of MultiCraft. A long article was published, which we summarise here.

A meeting will be held tomorrow to discuss budget cuts at MultiCraft. The cause: multiplayer, which is barely profitable, because MultiCraft pays for the hosting of thousands of servers without generating enough revenue. The Russian community, one of the largest, has been particularly loss-making since the 2022 sanctions. Indeed, because of the war in Ukraine and the sanctions imposed on Russia, Russians no longer make purchases in the game, even though they are MultiCraft's biggest players.

The recent appearance of a cheating software has made the situation worse: funding an anti-cheat would be an extra investment, on top of the colossal losses the game is already taking. Russian servers would be the first affected, and all of multiplayer could follow within a few months.

Note that nothing has been decided yet.
