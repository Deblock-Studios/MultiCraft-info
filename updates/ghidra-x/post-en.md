It all started when a [Telegram](https://t.me/mcrcheat) channel introduced Ghidra X, a cheat client for MultiCraft.
On the channel, several videos show the client in action: so this is not a scam.

Among the features are an ESP, a noclip, a fly, a killaura, an XRay and many others, all fully working in multiplayer. Several Telegram channels covering MultiCraft news relayed the information, to the point that MoNTE48 spoke on [this channel](https://t.me/novosty_multicraft) around 5:40pm, saying that anyone could get hacked with AI today.

Around 8:10pm, the cheat's creator shared a screenshot of a message sent by MoNTE48:

> Thank you. Multiplayer modes will most likely be completely closed in Russia. Congratulations

The cheat's developer answered by mocking MultiCraft and pointing at their inability to build a robust anti-cheat system.
He also announced that he may have found new ways to raid servers (bringing in a lot of fake players for malicious purposes).

Around 8:40pm, the cheat's developer announced that MultiCraft hardened its security: the app now checks whether it runs on a phone or a computer, and servers disconnect players on computers.

The cheat's developer then announced that he will "release Ghidra's resources" once he reaches 100 subscribers, or tomorrow at 7pm. He claims the MultiCraft team will patch their client, which will not stop people from getting around their blocks with their own methods, and that it will take MultiCraft much longer to block everything.

The developer already distributes the cheat client for free on his Telegram channel for Android, MacOS and Windows.

We will keep you informed as the situation evolves.
