The Halloween update is here!
Last year’s Halloween items are back, along with 4 new ones available only in the shop.

The new items are:
- A table candlestick, one block high (~20 corners)
- A floor chandeier, two blocks high (~25 corners)
- A skeleton candle (~20 corners)
- A decoration with a broom in leaves (~15 blocks)

The Halloween background of the menu was removed in the face of strong criticism from players. The background was indeed generated by AI and did not look like MultiCraft.
