The Halloween update is here!
Last year's Halloween items are back, along with 4 new little ones available only in the shop.

The new items are:
- A table candle holder, one block tall (~20 coins)
- A floor candle holder, two blocks tall (~25 coins)
- A skeleton candle (~20 coins)
- A decoration with a broom among leaves (~15 coins)

The Halloween menu background has been removed following strong criticism from players. The background was indeed AI-generated and didn't look like MultiCraft at all.
