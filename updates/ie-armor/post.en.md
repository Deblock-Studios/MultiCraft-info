# Do `/ie armor`
then click on the armor option
then choose the piece of armor you want. Then paint your armor!
You can choose
* durability,
* protection,
* healing
* or a bonus in speed, jump or gravity..
(you can also modify things to use it as an item... (the effect category will only change things on the item itself))
