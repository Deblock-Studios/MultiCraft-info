You've been waiting for it, we've all been waiting for it: block creation is officially available! For a while now we've been able to create our blocks officially with a super complete system! you can paint each FACE of the block, you can choose its destruction speed, the block's sound, whether it has rotations, whether it falls, whether you want to turn it into stairs etc..., whether it's bouncy, whether it deals damage, and whether it conducts bluestone, you can also choose its maximum stack, its cooking time, and its light source! all while doing "/ie block"
