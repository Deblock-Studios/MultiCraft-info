We have some vehicle updates!
- Buttons have been added to every vehicle to let you get inside. Pressing and holding on the vehicle will now open its inventory. However, we still don't have a release date for the standard servers.

- The skin editor is now available on all beta servers! It allows you to create a custom skin for 100 coins. Again, there is no release date yet for the standard servers.
