---
date: 2026-09-14
title: The game switch to Vulkan 1.3
---

According to Yota, a reliable source for MultiCraft, the game has switched its graphics engine to Vulkan 1.3. In practical terms, this move promises up to twice the frame rate (FPS) while causing your device to heat up less!
However, another channel, Infos MultiCraft, offers a more nuanced perspective, noting that this change entails development costs and that some devices may consequently no longer be able to run the game. MoNTE48, the game's owner, also adds a caveat, stating that the performance boost depends on the game mode; in multiplayer, frame rate drops are reportedly much more frequent.
