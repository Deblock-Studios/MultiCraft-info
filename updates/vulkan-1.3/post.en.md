---
date: 2026-09-20
title: The game moves to Vulkan 1.3
---

According to Yota, a reliable source from MultiCraft, the game has changed its graphics engine to Vulkan 1.3. Concretely, this switch would offer up to 2x more FPS while making your device heat up less!
Another channel, Infos MultiCraft, nevertheless adds nuance by pointing out that this change represents a development cost and that some devices may therefore now be unable to run the game. MoNTE48, the game's owner, also adds nuance by announcing that the improvement depends on the game mode. In multiplayer, FPS drops would be much more frequent.
