Vehicles are now craftable!

The recipe (mainly for the helicopter) is quite complex. The vehicles remain available in the shop.

To operate them, you need to crouch and click to add fuel (coal). Then the vehicle will start!

As you can see in the images and videos, we mainly tested the helicopter.

It can fly without size limitations, allows us to move in all directions, and can also hover in the air.

It doesn't have a passenger seat, but you can carry a passenger. The person simply needs to be on the helicopter and jump repeatedly.

We will keep you informed about their arrival on the live servers!
