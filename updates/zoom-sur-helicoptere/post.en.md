Vehicles are now craftable!
The recipe (mainly for the helicopter) is very complex. Vehicles also remain available in the shop.

To get them running, you need to crouch + click to put fuel in them (= coal). Then the vehicle can start!

As you can see in the images and videos, we mostly tested the helicopter.
It can fly with no size limit, lets us move in every direction, and can also hover in mid-air.
It doesn't have a passenger seat, but you can carry a passenger on top of it. The person just has to stand on the helicopter and jump repeatedly.

We'll keep you posted on their arrival on the normal servers!
